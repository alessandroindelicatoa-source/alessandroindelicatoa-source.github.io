01

Profile

I study how attitudes towards immigration form, what role national identity and religion play in them, and how climate and risk shape people's mobility.

I work with European surveys, open data and administrative sources, combining econometrics with multi-criteria decision methods and fuzzy logic. I have built some of these methods into my own software, which I reuse across projects. Before joining the Universidade de Vigo I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Universidad de Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, where I still teach on a master's programme.

02

Research

Attitudes towards immigration

What drives openness towards immigrants and refugees in Europe: party vote, national identity, values, economic perceptions and subjective well-being. Migration discourse on social media.

National identity and religion

Facets of national identity, tolerance and fundamentalism, attitudes towards other religions and civil rights. Economic doctrine in papal encyclicals.

Climate, risk and migration

Net migration and climate anomalies in Gran Canaria, perception of weather warnings in the Canary Islands, and attitudes towards climate change and climate neutrality in Europe.

Mobility, transport and territory

Homeworking and travel demand, transport user satisfaction in Central Europe, transport investment preferences, low-carbon urban regeneration and gastronomic tourism in Vigo.

Software

Apps I have built for research and teaching.

FQ

Fuzzy Quadrant Mapper

Python · Streamlit

Implements Fuzzy Hybrid TOPSIS, the Fuzzy Clustering ECO-Extended Apostle Model and conditional probability ratios. It places survey respondents in the quadrants of the Apostle model (Apostle, Mercenary, Hostage, Defector).

  • Fuzzy Hybrid TOPSIS
  • ECO-Extended Apostle Model
  • Conditional probability ratios

Used in several papers on attitudes towards immigrants, national identity and religion.

Open the app ↗
PM

PerspectiveMapper

Python · Streamlit

A discourse analysis app for text corpora. It explores the topics, tone and framing of a set of documents and compares texts with one another.

  • Topic modelling
  • Sentiment analysis
  • Bias detection
  • Document similarity

Currently used in a study of economic doctrine in papal encyclicals.
